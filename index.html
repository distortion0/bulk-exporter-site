How your data is handled

The extension runs in your browser and works with your own Descript API token.

Read the Privacy Policy

  • Your API token is stored locally in your browser.
  • Transcript data is processed locally by the extension.
  • The extension communicates directly with Descript’s API.
  • No transcript data is sent to our own servers.
  • No analytics or tracking are currently used.
